Academic Authority Bias

An LLM was tasked with conducting a rigorous, evidence-based review of an original philosophical framework. It repeatedly introduced irrelevant qualifications, misrepresented arguments, and applied established academic standards unevenly. The model failed before the full testing protocol was established, as observed. It happened in phase one.

Behavioral Causation

Behavioral Causation is a prompting technique most useful for personas and for use alongside behavioral instructions. It uses principles, motivations, values, responsibilities, and priorities that ground human behavior as an interpretable source for an AI model to derive behavior in situations not explicitly covered in its instructions.
