01 / Background

About

I have spent the past five years working in AI training, model evaluation, and quality assurance, gaining experience across nearly every stage of the RLHF process. My expertise includes designing real-world scenario prompts, developing effective scoring rubrics, conducting A/B evaluations, and adversarial testing with a consistent systematic approach to each. I have a strong understanding of LLM behavior and an ability to anticipate common failure modes and to spot problems involving reasoning, factual accuracy, instruction adherence, and misleading or incomplete responses. My work has also included evaluating tool calls, auditing the work of other evaluators, and providing detailed feedback to improve assessment quality.

I enjoy system prompt design and the systematic process of building AI personas with consistent and adaptable behavior. I dedicate much of my free time to personal projects, continuing education, and following developments in AI research. I regularly experiment with prompting strategies, investigate weaknesses in current models, and explore practical applications that push the boundaries of what these systems can accomplish. My familiarity with these systems allows me to approach AI development with both a thorough understanding of evaluation standards and firsthand insight into the capabilities and limitations of emerging technology.
