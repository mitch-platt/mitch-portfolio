01 / Background

About

I have spent the past five years working in AI training, model evaluation, and quality assurance, gaining experience across nearly every stage of the RLHF process. My expertise includes prompt engineering, developing detailed scoring rubrics, conducting A/B evaluations, adversarial testing, and analyzing model failures. I have a strong understanding of LLM behavior and an ability to recognize subtle problems involving reasoning, factual accuracy, instruction adherence, and misleading or incomplete responses. My work has also included designing complex environments to test AI agents, evaluating tool use and decision-making, auditing the work of other evaluators, and providing detailed feedback to improve assessment quality. I have an interest in system prompt design and the systematic process of building AI personas with consistent and adaptable behavior.

My involvement with AI extends well beyond my professional responsibilities. I dedicate much of my free time to personal projects, continuing education, and following developments in AI research. I regularly experiment with prompting strategies, investigate weaknesses in current models, and explore practical applications that push the boundaries of what these systems can accomplish. This combination of professional experience and independent experimentation allows me to approach AI development with both a thorough understanding of evaluation standards and firsthand insight into the capabilities and limitations of emerging technology.
